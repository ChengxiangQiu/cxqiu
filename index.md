### Research Interests
Chengxiang (CX) Qiu is a computational biologist specializing in single-cell transcriptomics and developmental biology. Building on his expertise in single-cell genomics, human disease genetics, and mammalian development, Chengxiang's lab aims to investigate how cell differentiation and lineage commitment are orchestrated through the regulation of gene expression, with the goal of uncovering the molecular and cellular mechanisms underlying both normal human development and developmental disorders, particularly neurodevelopmental disorders.
